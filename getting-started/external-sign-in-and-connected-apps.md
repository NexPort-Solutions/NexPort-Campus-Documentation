# External Sign-In and Connected Apps

Use this guide when your organization asks you to sign in with an external provider such as Google or Microsoft Entra ID.

## Sign In with an Organization Provider

1. Open your campus sign-in page.
2. Select your organization sign-in option (for example, Google or Microsoft Entra ID).
3. Complete the provider prompts.
4. Return to NexPort Campus after authorization completes.

> Tip
> If your campus also shows NexPort username/password sign-in, use the method required by your organization policy.

## Common Sign-In Messages and What to Do

### Access denied by your organization

This usually means your account is not currently allowed by organization sign-in policy.

- Confirm you selected the correct organization sign-in option.
- Try signing out of other provider accounts and signing in again with the correct account.
- Contact your organization administrator if the message continues.

### Provider is temporarily unavailable

This usually means the external provider did not complete authorization.

- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Confirm your browser allows pop-ups for sign-in.
- Try a private/incognito browser session.

### Your sign-in could not be completed

This can happen when required account details are missing or outdated.

- Retry sign-in and complete any required profile/consent prompts.
- If it still fails, contact support and include the time of the attempt and the message shown.

## Connected Apps and Revoking Access

You can remove previously granted access from your provider account security settings.

- Revoking access removes the existing connection between your provider account and NexPort Campus.
- The next time you sign in, you may be asked to grant access again.
- If your organization policy changed, sign-in may remain blocked until an administrator updates your access.

## When to Contact Support

Contact support when:

- You can sign in to your provider but cannot finish sign-in to NexPort Campus.
- You receive repeated access denied messages after using the correct account.
- You revoked and re-authorized access but still cannot sign in.

Use the support guidance in [Guidelines for Support](guidelines-for-support.md).
