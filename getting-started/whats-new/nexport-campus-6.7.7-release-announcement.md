# NexPort Campus 6.7.7 Release Announcement

### **Enhancing the Learning Experience with Key Updates**

The latest **NexPort Campus 6.7.7** update has officially launched! Released at **2:00 AM** today, this milestone brings critical improvements to **SCORM 2004 compatibility, administrative tools, and system performance**. Our ongoing mission is to **streamline online learning**, ensuring an intuitive and effective platform for administrators, instructors, and learners alike.

***

### **🌟 What's New in NexPort Campus 6.7.7?**

#### **📚 Expanded SCORM 2004 & Enhanced CMI Model Support**

* **Seamless SCORM 2004 Courseware Management**: Administrators can now **upload, track, and manage** SCORM 2004 content with enhanced functionality, including improvements to the CMI model for better data tracking.
* **SCORM Data Accessibility**: Admins gain direct access to **view and edit SCORM 2004 attempt data**, benefiting from enhanced CMI model support for improved troubleshooting and progress tracking.
* **SCORM Quiz Error Fixes**: Resolved **quiz submission errors** to improve assessment reliability.

#### **🛠️ Instructor & Administrator Enhancements**

* **Instructor Grade Adjustments**: Instructors now have the ability to **modify issued grades**, providing greater flexibility in grading.
* **User Deletion Audit Logging**: Improved transparency and tracking of **deleted user records**.
* **SCORM Courseware Stability Fixes**: Ensured **consistent course launch functionality** for SCORM 2004 assignments.

#### **🎯 User Experience & Performance Improvements**

* **Improved Course Completion Messaging**: Students receive **clearer confirmation messages** upon finishing assignments.
* **Faster Classroom Navigation**: Addressed performance issues when **returning to the classroom**.
* **Enhanced Internal Mail Handling**: Fixed issues related to **special character handling in internal mail headers**.

#### **🚀 Infrastructure & API Upgrades**

* **Subscription API Enhancements**: Organization details are now included in **subscription lookup responses**.
* **Database Optimization**: Implemented **missing index migrations** to improve system performance and reduce query times.
* **Fixed Fulfillment Job Errors**: Ensured **reliable processing of training plan requirements**.

***

### **🔎 What This Means for You**

With NexPort Campus 6.7.7, **learning delivery is smoother, faster, and more intuitive than ever**. These updates empower instructors and administrators with better tools, ensuring **a seamless training experience for learners**.
