# NexPort Campus 6.7.5 Release Announcement

NexPort Solutions proudly unveils Version 6.7.5 of NexPort Campus, delivering targeted enhancements to enrich training plan management and user navigation. This update, developed with input from our community and in-depth analysis, focuses on clarifying training plan prerequisites for a more intuitive user experience.

**Enhanced Prerequisite Visibility and Management**

* **Clarity for Students**: Building on NexPort Campus's robust training plan features, Version 6.7.5 introduces enhanced visibility of prerequisite chains for students. Learners can now easily identify the requirements needed to begin any training plan, fostering a more guided and informed educational journey.
*   **Developer Tools Upgraded**: Training plan developers gain streamlined access to prerequisite information within their design environment, simplifying the process of creating coherent and structured learning paths. This update is crucial for ensuring educational content is both accessible and logically sequenced.\


    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (58).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Prerequisite display in the Training Plan Editor</p></figcaption></figure>
*   **Administrator Insight**: Enrollment administrators are provided with a comprehensive view of the prerequisite framework for each training plan, enabling better support and guidance for students navigating their educational pathways.\


    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (57).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Prerequisites visible from the Academic Override Area</p></figcaption></figure>

**Key Bug Fixes and Improvements**

* **Fixed Loading Dialog Issue**: Addressing user feedback, this update resolves an issue where the modal loading dialog persisted across certain platform sections, enhancing the overall smoothness and responsiveness of the NexPort Campus experience.
*   **Academic Override User Display**: Enhancements in displaying Academic Override users provide administrators with clearer, more contextual information, improving administrative workflows and decision-making processes.\

**Aligning with Educational Goals**

These updates reflect our commitment to enhancing NexPort Campus's functionality, aligning closely with the needs of administrators, developers, and learners. By focusing on the usability and clarity of training plan prerequisites, we're streamlining the educational process, making it easier for users to navigate their learning journeys effectively.

**Next Steps for Users**

We encourage the NexPort Campus community to explore these new features and improvements. Your feedback is crucial as we continue to refine our platform to better serve your educational needs. For more details on this update and for support, please visit our Help Center or reach out to our support team.
