# NexPort Campus 6.7.9 Release Announcement

NexPort Solutions is excited to announce the release of **NexPort Campus 6.7.9**, the latest update to our flagship learning and knowledge management system. This release focuses on three critical areas: improving enrollment handling for open-ended products, enabling real-time integration through webhook triggers, and addressing NMLS compliance issues with course naming.

***

### 🔑 Key Enhancements in 6.7.9

#### 1. Smarter Enrollment Redemptions

Managing open-ended courseware just got easier. In previous versions, redeeming invoices for open-ended products could lead to inconsistencies if the syllabus wasn’t properly linked. With 6.7.9, the system now leverages syllabus information from the product itself to restart enrollments reliably. This ensures smoother renewals and prevents enrollment failures—even when a new syllabus is introduced mid-cycle.

**Benefit for Admins:** No more manual intervention for failed redemptions. The system intelligently aligns invoices with the correct syllabus for a seamless student experience.

***

#### 2. Webhook Triggers for Account Creation

Integrations just became more powerful. A new **webhook trigger** has been added for account creation events, making it possible to connect NexPort Campus with external systems in real-time. From automated CRM updates to custom analytics pipelines, this feature opens new doors for workflow automation.

**Benefit for Partners:** Reduce manual data entry, accelerate onboarding, and expand your integration capabilities with just a few clicks.

***

#### 3. Compliance Fix: NMLS Course Naming

Several training providers flagged an issue where NMLS rejected course approvals due to an extra “classroom” tag appended to course names. NexPort Campus 6.7.9 resolves this issue, ensuring course naming conventions now meet NMLS approval standards.

**Benefit for Providers:** Faster approval times and fewer compliance headaches when submitting courses for verification.

***

### 🌟 Why This Release Matters

Together, these updates address real-world partner needs: smoother student enrollment, tighter integrations, and regulatory compliance. NexPort’s mission has always been to empower education providers with tools that reduce friction and accelerate delivery.
