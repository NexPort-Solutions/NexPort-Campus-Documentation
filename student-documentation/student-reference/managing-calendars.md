# Managing Calendars (Student)

Use the Manage Calendars slide‑out to control which organization calendars appear alongside your personal calendar.

- Open the Manage Calendars slide‑out from the Calendar page.
- Search by organization name; click Pin to add it to your view.
- Toggle Include descendants to include events from the organization and its child organizations.
- Unpin to remove a calendar from your view.

Notes
- Your ability to see or pin a calendar depends on your permissions or subscriptions.
- Pinned calendars are remembered for your device and can be adjusted anytime.
