# Tenant Management Overview

Tenant management governs how NexPort Campus organizes billing, entitlements, and
administration across one or more organizations. Each organization belongs to a
tenant, and in most cases a full organization tree rolls up to the same tenant.
The tenant is the top-tier billing unit that owns feature entitlements and
billing rules for every organization under it. This area is separate from
Invoice Management, which focuses on creating and viewing invoices for
instructional content.

## Tenant and organization examples

- **Single company with internal divisions:** A single tenant represents the
  company. Each division is its own organization (and may have child
  organizations). All divisions inherit billing and feature entitlements from
  the tenant.
- **Provider with multiple customer organizations:** A single tenant represents
  the provider. Each customer has its own organization tree under that tenant.
  Billing and entitlements are controlled at the tenant level and apply to each
  customer organization.

Tenant management typically involves:

- Tenant profiles that group one or more organizations (often a full org tree)
  into a single billing context.
- Tenant billing accounts and rules that determine which features or services
  are included or billable.
- Tenant workspace tools used by Billing Managers and SysOps to review tenant
  settings, entitlements, and billing activity.
- Organization dashboards that surface the tenant profile linked to the selected
  organization.

## Who uses tenant management

Tenant management is intended for NexPort staff roles such as Billing Managers
and SysOps. Organization administrators may see tenant-related information, but
billing settings are controlled by authorized NexPort staff.

## Accessing tenant tools

1. Open the **Tenant Workspace** from the Admin tools menu.
2. Use the **Overview** blade to review tenant profile and organization details.
3. Navigate to the billing, entitlements, and invoice views as needed.

If you are working from **Manage Campus**, select an organization and use the
**Tenant Information** card on the organization dashboard to jump to the
associated tenant profile.
