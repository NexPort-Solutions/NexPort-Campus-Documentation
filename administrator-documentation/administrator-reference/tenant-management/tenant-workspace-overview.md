# Tenant Workspace Overview

The Tenant Workspace is the primary place for Billing Managers and SysOps to
review tenant settings, entitlements, and billing activity. It is designed to
manage tenants that span one or more organizations.

## What you can do here

- Review the tenant profile and root organization.
- Inspect feature entitlements and billing rules.
- Navigate to tenant billing views and invoice history.

## Accessing the workspace

1. Open **Administration**.
2. Choose **Tenant Workspace**.
3. Use the search or list view to select the tenant you want to manage.

## Related views

- **Tenant Profile**: Edit tenant identity and billing context.
- **Tenant Features & Entitlements**: Review what is included or billable.
- **Billing Rules**: Control how charges are applied across the tenant.
