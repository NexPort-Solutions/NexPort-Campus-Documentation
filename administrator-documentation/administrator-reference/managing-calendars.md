# Managing Calendars (Admin)

Add organization calendars you manage to your view and optionally include descendant organizations.

- Open the Manage Calendars slide‑out.
- Search by organization; Pin calendars you manage.
- Enable Include descendants to show events from the org and its children.
- Unpin to remove a calendar from your view.

Notes
- Visibility and actions are governed by your permissions.
- Pinned selections are remembered per user/device and can be updated anytime.
